2. Verifica puerto vacio:

3. Repite prueba.

==================================================
SECCION C2 - BACKFILL HISTORICO (DESDE 2025-01-01)
==================================================

Ejecuta en servidor interno (PowerShell):

$envFile = "C:\contpaq-sync\.env"
$script = "C:\contpaq-sync\contpaq_sync_agent.py"

$fechas = @(
	"2026-03-01",
	"2026-01-01",
	"2025-10-01",
	"2025-07-01",
	"2025-04-01",
	"2025-01-01"
)

foreach ($f in $fechas) {
	(Get-Content $envFile) -replace '^CONTPAQ_START_DATE=.*$', "CONTPAQ_START_DATE=$f" | Set-Content $envFile -Encoding ascii
	Write-Host "===> Backfill desde $f"
	py -3 $script
	Start-Sleep -Seconds 8
}

Al terminar el backfill, deja fecha operativa diaria (recomendada):

(Get-Content C:\contpaq-sync\.env) -replace '^CONTPAQ_START_DATE=.*$', 'CONTPAQ_START_DATE=2026-03-01' | Set-Content C:\contpaq-sync\.env -Encoding ascii
Get-Content C:\contpaq-sync\.env
